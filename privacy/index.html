Privacy

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts and album covers are served with the site. Listening links open an external service only when you choose them. MP3 samples are served from the Hans Gál and Simon Fox-Gál audio archives on GitHub Pages. Audio is requested only when you choose to play; subsequent samples play in sequence until you pause or close the player. The audio host receives normal web request information.

Browsing the catalogue

Search terms and filters appear in the page address so that a catalogue view can be shared. Your place in the catalogue is stored in session storage in your browser, to restore it when you return from an album. It is not sent to a separate analytics service and normally expires when the tab is closed.

Contact enquiries

The contact form uses FormSubmit to deliver your name, email address, project details and message to Simon. These details are used to respond to your enquiry. FormSubmit states that it retains submissions for 30 days; its own privacy policy applies to the delivery service. The recipient email address is not included in the public website.

The form reports whether a message was sent and keeps your text if delivery fails.

Please do not send confidential audio, access credentials or sensitive documents through the contact form.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard web request information under GitHub’s privacy policy.
