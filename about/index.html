About

Tonmeister and recording producer

Simon Fox-Gál

Simon Fox-Gál is a Tonmeister and recording producer. He works with artists as a fellow musician, bringing an independent ear and musical judgement to the decisions that shape the recorded performance.

He trained as a Tonmeister at the Hochschule für Musik in Detmold and worked under the tutelage of Philips producer Volker Straus. His work integrates musicianship, psychological awareness and technical expertise, with choices of recorded sound, editing and mastering serving the musical interpretation. His starting point is to listen: to become familiar with the artists’ interpretation and the way they work together. The musical exchange develops from there, with the degree of involvement differing from one collaboration to another.

His work has particularly focused on chamber music, baroque repertoire, classical symphonic repertoire and contemporary orchestral works. Long-standing collaborations included Antonio Meneses, La Serenissima, the Kamus Quartet and the Ostrobothnian Chamber Orchestra, alongside recordings with Royal Northern Sinfonia and the BBC National Orchestra of Wales. Sakari Oramo and Thomas Zehetmair are among the conductors in this body of work; Zehetmair also appears as a soloist.

The grandson of composer Hans Gál, Simon grew up in a musical family and played chamber music regularly from a young age.

His aim: recordings that draw the listener in, explore the music’s possibilities and have something distinctive to say.

Recognition for the recordings

Recordings in the catalogue have received two Gramophone Awards, several Finnish Emma Awards and a GRAMMY nomination. These distinctions recognise the recordings and their credited artists.

  • 2024 Emma — Finnish Accordion Concertos · Ostrobothnian Chamber Orchestra
  • 2017 Gramophone, Baroque Instrumental — The Italian Job · Adrian Chandler & La Serenissima
  • 2016 Emma — Side by Side · Kreeta-Maria Kentala
  • 2014 Emma — C. P. E. Bach: Hamburger Sinfonien · Ostrobothnian Chamber Orchestra & Sakari Oramo
  • 2013 GRAMMY-nominated recording — Gál & Elgar: Cello Concertos · Antonio Meneses
  • 2012 Emma — Seppo Pohjola: String Quartets · Kamus Quartet
  • 2010 Gramophone, Baroque Instrumental — The French Connection · Adrian Chandler & La Serenissima
  • 2010 Emma — Bach · Ismo Eskelinen
  • 2006 Emma — Minds and Moods · Tampere Philharmonic Orchestra

Years identify the award chronology, which can differ from release dates. The 2024 Emma was presented in March 2025.
