Credits

Album covers belong to the respective record labels, artists and artwork rights holders. Covers are displayed complete, with their original proportions.

Recording information is drawn from the supplied portfolio, label release pages and booklets, artist and ensemble catalogues, and AllMusic. Production, engineering, editing, mixing and mastering credits retain their distinct meanings.

Artists listed on a recording do not necessarily appear on every work. Work-level performer relationships are shown only where documented. Different editions can have different release dates; a reissue is not a new production.

Artwork sources and provenance are retained in separate project documentation.
