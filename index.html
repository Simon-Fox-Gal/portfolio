Why I make recordings

Working together

In the early part of a session, I tend to stay in the background. It gives your interpretation room to unfold and gives me time to hear how you work. As the session develops, I get more closely involved where the music calls for it.

I enjoy being someone you can turn to for a detailed musical exchange, or simply rely on to be listening carefully. Hearing a performance take shape at close quarters is a privilege; hearing its individuality come through in the finished recording is a lasting pleasure.

I work in both English and German.

Simon
